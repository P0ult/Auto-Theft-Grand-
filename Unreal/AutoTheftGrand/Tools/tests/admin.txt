# Free roam's admin tools (run with -ATGMenu): the console (`) runs help, a car and the weather; the Admin tab's
# actions answer in the line at the top; the Vehicles tab summons a plane in the air.
wait 4
ATG.Menu FreeRoam
wait 2
ATG.Input Tilde 1
wait 0.1
ATG.Input Tilde 0
wait 0.3
ATG.Type help
ATG.SlateKey Enter
ATG.Type car zenith
ATG.SlateKey Enter
wait 0.5
shot admin_console
ATG.SlateKey Escape
wait 0.3
ATG.State
ATG.Console weather rain
ATG.Console wanted 2
ATG.Menu Pause
wait 0.5
ATG.Menu Page Admin
ATG.Menu Do Act:heal
ATG.Menu Do Density:traffic:heavy
wait 0.3
shot admin_tab
ATG.Menu Page Vehicles
ATG.Menu Do Spawn:skipper
wait 1
ATG.State
shot admin_plane
quit
