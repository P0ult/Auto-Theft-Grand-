# The Online tab and chat (run with -ATGMenu while node server.mjs runs on localhost:8080): Multiplayer on the
# title starts free roam with the pause menu on its Online tab. Join a room by its code, chat from the tab, then
# the in-game chat box (/), and leave.
wait 4
ATG.Menu Multiplayer
wait 2
ATG.MenuCheck open 1
ATG.MenuCheck tab Online
shot online_offline
ATG.Menu Type name Tester
ATG.Menu Type code atgtest
ATG.Menu Do NetJoin
wait 1.5
ATG.Net
shot online_joined
ATG.Menu Type say hello from the tab
ATG.Menu Do NetSend
ATG.Menu Do NetColor:3
wait 0.5
shot online_chat
ATG.Menu Resume
wait 1
ATG.Input Slash 1
wait 0.1
ATG.Input Slash 0
wait 0.3
ATG.Type hello from the box
shot online_chatbox
ATG.SlateKey Enter
wait 0.5
shot online_feed
ATG.Net
ATG.Net leave
wait 0.5
ATG.Net
quit
