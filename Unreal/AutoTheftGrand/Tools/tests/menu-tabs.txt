# The pause menu's tabs in free roam (run with -ATGMenu): M opens the Map tab, a right-click on the map sets a
# waypoint, then a shot of every tab, and the title screen again after Quit.
wait 4
shot tabs_title
ATG.Menu FreeRoam
wait 2
ATG.Input M 1
wait 0.1
ATG.Input M 0
wait 0.5
ATG.MenuCheck open 1
ATG.MenuCheck tab Map
ATG.MapWaypoint 0.75 0.5
wait 0.5
shot tabs_map
ATG.Menu Tab 1
wait 0.3
shot tabs_teleport
ATG.Menu Tab 1
wait 0.3
shot tabs_vehicles
ATG.Menu Tab 1
wait 0.3
shot tabs_admin
ATG.Menu Tab 1
wait 0.3
shot tabs_online
ATG.Menu Tab 1
wait 0.3
shot tabs_stats
ATG.Menu Tab 1
wait 0.3
shot tabs_settings
ATG.Menu Tab 1
wait 0.3
shot tabs_controls
ATG.Menu Quit
wait 3
shot tabs_title_again
quit
